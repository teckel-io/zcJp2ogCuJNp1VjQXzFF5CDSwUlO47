Benedict can’t start yet — he needs teckel credits. Add or top up your teckel credits in the teckel App, then try again. If you have just paid, starting Benedict is not instant: allow up to about ten minutes from the moment your payment clears. Your forms and records are unaffected.
Migrate your account in the teckel App — today, not on the day you need it. Migrating saves your account to an email address and a passphrase you choose — write the passphrase down somewhere the practice controls. Once it is saved, a lost phone, a new phone or a reinstall is something you can recover from, and you do not need the old phone to do it. Until then, a reinstall creates a fresh, empty account and your practice’s records are not reachable from it. Open the App, go to Accounts, tap Migrate account, and choose Migrate FROM this device.
